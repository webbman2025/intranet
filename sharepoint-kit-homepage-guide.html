SharePoint Kit 2 — Homepage layout guide

What each band is for, why it sits where it does, and how it maps to the legacy HTHK intranet and Modern SharePoint building blocks. Use with the live mock in figma_ai/ (SharePoint Kit 2 · full-width hero).

Audience: product, comms, SharePoint dev Max content width 1204px (hero full bleed on Kit 2) Mobile: hero carousel · horizontal app tiles

Centre panel: live SharePoint Kit 2 mock (scroll, search, hero carousel on narrow widths). Requires npm run dev:local in figma_ai/, or a built copy at figma_ai/dist/.

1

Microsoft 365 suite bar

What
Global M365 chrome: app launcher, SharePoint label, search (“Ask HTHK AI”), profile.
Why it exists
Staff expect the same shell as other M365 apps; search is the primary “ask anything” entry.
Why here (top)
Fixed/sticky at the top—standard for SharePoint Online and hub sites; never scrolls away.
SharePoint: suite bar + search (Copilot-ready copy in mock)
2

Left app rail

What
Icon rail (Home, Viva Engage-style spark, files, people)—M365 wayfinding.
Why
Reinforces “you are in the Microsoft ecosystem,” not a standalone legacy frame.
Why here
Desktop only; hidden on mobile to save horizontal space.
SharePoint: global nav / app bar pattern
3

Site header & hub navigation

What
HTHK Intranet title, member count, Follow, and links: Home, My tools, Staff announcements, Staff services, Policies & forms, Learning & community.
Why
Replaces legacy top frame + dept menu with task-based hubs instead of organisation chart.
Why here
Immediately under M365 bar—defines site identity before any content; nav anchors jump to on-page sections.
SharePoint: site header + hub navigation
4

Personal bar (“For you, Alex”)

What
Personalisation status, AI trust line, link to personalise the page.
Why
Sets expectation for targeted home experience and responsible AI (grounded in HTHK sources).
Why here
Between nav and hero—brief context before featured content; full width on Kit 2.
SharePoint: personalisation / Viva-style messaging (optional web part)
5

Featured stories hero

What
Five-tile hero (one large featured story + four tiles). Kit 2: full viewport width. Mobile: swipe carousel with dots.
Why
Carries corporate priorities (anniversary, dinner, culture, sustainability, security training)—modern comms site pattern.
Why here
First visual impact after personal bar; answers “what matters now?” before tools and lists.
SharePoint: Hero web part (multi-tile layout may need custom SPFx or creative layout)
6

Online applications

What
Quick links: HCM, Webmail, Directory, eLearning, e-Form, IT desk, Room booking, Benefits.
Why
Direct successor to legacy Online Applications— highest-frequency daily actions.
Why here
Directly under hero: “read news → do work.” Desktop grid; mobile horizontal rounded tiles.
SharePoint: Quick links web part
7

Staff announcements

What
Three highlight cards: staff offers, new colleagues, CEO message—with image, type, date, CTA.
Why
Centralises “must know” HR/comms/leadership items; more prominent than a simple link list.
Why here
After apps: urgent/important messages before the longer news feed.
SharePoint: News / Highlighted content web parts
8

What’s hot

What
Home teaser → article; See all, topic badge, or hub nav News → site news page: title bar, topic filters (sidebar desktop / scroll chips mobile), promoted story + 3-column card grid with excerpt and metadata.
Why
Legacy What’s Hot—ongoing updates; topics = news metadata / publishing teams.
Why here
Below announcements: deeper news feed after time-sensitive highlights.
SharePoint: News web part (home) + News site page / filtered view
9

HTHK Mobile (app download)

What
Promo for intranet on phone: benefits list, App Store / Play CTAs, phone mock + QR placeholder.
Why
Single place for mobile story (responsive site + optional app). Replaces a removed duplicate banner.
Why here
Mid-page break after news—staff who scroll for content see mobile option once; devs should align copy with real app (SharePoint app vs custom HTHK app).
SharePoint: Text + button / Image web parts; store links if applicable
10

Staff essentials

What
Filmstrip: shuttle bus, cafeteria menu, Staff Jetso offers.
Why
High-traffic operational info from legacy intranet (admin, facilities, benefits).
Why here
After mobile block—practical “everyday” links before formal policies.
SharePoint: Quick links + images or Highlighted content
11

Trusted policies and forms

What
Grid of handbook, expenses, security policies, HCM guide, typhoon policy, medical benefits.
Why
Legacy popular documents; emphasises governed central repository after migration.
Why here
Mid-lower page—important but not first click; compliance content after tasks and news.
SharePoint: Document library links / Quick links
12

Company events

What
Upcoming events with date badge, title, time/location, view link.
Why
Supports participation and internal comms; complements news with dated commitments.
Why here
Lower third—planning horizon after policies; neutral background band for visual rhythm.
SharePoint: Events web part
13

People and culture

What
Large image + headline “Grow, connect and make a difference” + CTA to people & culture.
Why
Ties to hub nav “Learning & community”; emotional balance to policy/event blocks.
Why here
Before staff services grid—storytelling before structured browse tiles.
SharePoint: Hero / Image + text web part
14

Staff services (browse by need)

What
Six task hubs: Pay & benefits, Working at HTHK, Learning, Workplace services, Wellbeing, IT.
Why
Replaces dept-by-dept navigation with jobs to be done—core revamp idea.
Why here
Near bottom: power users and explorers; deep links to associated sites/libraries.
SharePoint: Hub links / Card layout web part
15

Footer

What
HTHK Intranet branding, privacy, directory, suggestion box, accessibility, help desk.
Why
Persistent trust, support, and legal links on every page.
Why here
Standard closure; teal band matches HTHK / SharePoint theme extension.
SharePoint: Footer placeholder / custom footer extension
